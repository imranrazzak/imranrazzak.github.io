The signal

One heartbeat, wave by wave Illustrative

Every heartbeat leaves the same sequence of waves on an ECG. Their shape, size and timing are what clinicians and models read.

P wave
The upper chambers (atria) activate.
QRS complex
The lower chambers (ventricles) activate. In adults it normally lasts under 120 ms.
T wave
The ventricles recover before the next beat.
PR interval
Start of the P wave to the start of the QRS complex, normally 120 to 200 ms.
QT interval
Start of the QRS complex to the end of the T wave. It shortens as the heart beats faster.

Rhythm

No two beats are the same length Illustrative

The time between successive R peaks, the RR interval, rises and falls with breathing and slower regulation of the circulation. This is heart rate variability. Move the heart rate control above to see the pattern shift.
