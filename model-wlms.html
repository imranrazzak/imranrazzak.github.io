WLMs Family

Open source models

Three models for three signals from the body

The WLMs family covers three signals recorded from the body over time: the electrocardiogram, continuous glucose monitoring and sleep. The model descriptions, results and release details will be added to each model page.

The models

ECGWM

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

The heart's electrical activity, beat by beat.

Model page → Model information coming soon

CGMWM

Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)

Glucose read by a wearable sensor every few minutes, day and night.

Model page → Model information coming soon

SleepWM

Sleep

How a night moves through wake, light, deep and REM sleep.

Model page → Model information coming soon
