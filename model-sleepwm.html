SleepWM is the WLMs model for sleep: how a night moves through wake, light, deep and REM sleep, and what the body does meanwhile. Its description, results and release details will be added here. Until then, this page shows what the signal looks like.

The signal

How the night divides between stages Illustrative

Sleep runs in cycles of roughly 90 minutes. Deep sleep (N3) is concentrated in the first cycles and REM sleep in the last ones, so the mix of stages says a lot about a night.

    Minutes and share of the eight hours in bed for the synthetic night shown above.

    Overnight physiology

    The body follows the stages Illustrative

    Heart rate falls as sleep deepens and becomes faster and less regular in REM sleep and around awakenings. Blood oxygen saturation stays in a narrow band, with small dips. The strip on top repeats the stages for the same night.
