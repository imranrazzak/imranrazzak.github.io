Teaching philosophy

I teach graduate-level courses that bridge research methodology and practical systems skills. My goal is to help students develop strong problem formulation, rigorous experimental design, reproducibility habits, and clear scientific communication—while also building the engineering foundations needed to deploy trustworthy AI in real-world settings.
