MedMO

Grounding and understanding multimodal large language model for medical images

MedMO unifies visual grounding, clinical reasoning and language understanding across medical imaging modalities. It is trained only on medical data, 26M+ samples from 45 datasets, and released in 4B and 8B sizes.

Why MedMO

Three limits of existing medical multimodal models

Multimodal large language models have advanced quickly, but their use in medicine is held back by limited domain coverage, imperfect alignment between modalities and too little grounded reasoning.

Reliance on distilled data

Most existing models are trained on data distilled from proprietary models, which often lacks the domain grounding needed for fine-grained clinical reasoning.

Hallucination risk

Distillation pipelines without structured supervision amplify hallucinations and inconsistencies in medical outputs.

Narrow modality coverage

Current models focus on single tasks or a few modalities instead of unified generalization across them.

Training

A four-stage training pipeline

MedMO is built on Qwen3-VL and post-trained in four stages. Each stage adds a capability: broad medical understanding, high-resolution localization, instruction following, and reward-driven grounding.

  1. General medical SFTLarge-scale training for foundational understanding18.5M samples · 768×768 images
  2. High-resolution SFTSpatial localization and fine-grained grounding3M samples · 1280×1280 images
  3. Instruction tuningHuman-style medical instruction following4.3M samples · Multi-task
  4. Reinforcement learningGRPO with verifiable rewards300K samples · Bounding-box IoU reward

Where the training samples go

  • Stage 1: General medical SFT 18.5M samples
  • Stage 2: High-resolution SFT 3M samples
  • Stage 3: Instruction tuning 4.3M samples
  • Stage 4: Reinforcement learning 300K samples

26.1M samples across the four stages. Stage 4 is 1% of the samples.

Cross-modal pretraining

Heterogeneous visual encoders are aligned with a medical language backbone using a DeepStack fusion mechanism.

Multi-task supervision

Training spans captioning, VQA, report generation, retrieval and grounded disease localization with bounding boxes.

Verifiable rewards

A bounding-box GIoU reward is combined with factuality checks to improve spatial grounding and step-by-step reasoning.

Scalable architecture

The modular design on Qwen3-VL allows later expansion to additional modalities.

Contributions

What MedMO contributes

Open-source foundation model

A post-trained multimodal vision-language model for medical image understanding and grounding, available in 4B and 8B variants.

Scalable training pipeline

26M+ multimodal medical samples curated from 45 datasets, with multi-stage post-training that progressively improves cross-modal alignment.

New evaluation benchmark

A dedicated Cell dataset built from open-source microscopy images with varying sizes, shapes and densities, for evaluating detection.

Comprehensive analysis

Experiments across data and methodology, providing an open benchmark for future multimodal medical LLM research.
