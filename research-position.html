Research Position

Research opportunities at MBZUAI

I welcome enquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers, visiting researchers, visiting students, and remote research interns interested in working with my group at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), Abu Dhabi.

Our interests include medical foundation models, vision–language models for clinical reasoning, causal discovery, and personalized healthcare AI.

Get in touch

Email imran.razzak@mbzuai.ac.ae with the subject Research Enquiry — [Role] — [Your Name].

  • Your CV, including research experience and publications.
  • A short description of your research interests and how they connect to our work.
  • Links to relevant papers, code, or projects.
  • Your intended role, availability, and proposed duration.

For MSc and PhD enquiries, please see Prospective Students.
