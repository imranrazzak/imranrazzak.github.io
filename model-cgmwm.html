CGMWM

Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)

CGMWM is the WLMs model for continuous glucose monitoring, where a wearable sensor reads glucose every few minutes, day and night. Its description, results and release details will be added here. Until then, this page shows what the signal looks like.

The signal

Fourteen days in one bar Illustrative

A CGM produces about 288 readings a day. The most common summary is time in range: the share of readings below, inside and above the target range.

    –Time in range across 14 days
    –Mean glucose
    –Coefficient of variation: variability relative to the mean

    Computed from the 14 synthetic days behind this page. The values describe this illustration only.

    Daily pattern

    The same 14 days, folded onto one clock Illustrative

    Stacking every day by time of day shows what is typical and what varies: the line is the median reading at each time, and the bands show how far individual days spread around it. This view is known as an ambulatory glucose profile.

    • Median
    • 25th to 75th percentile
    • 5th to 95th percentile
    • Target range limits
