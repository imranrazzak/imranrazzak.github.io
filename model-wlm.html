Wearable Language Models

World models for continuous physiological signals

Wearable Language Models (WLMs) are a model series for the signals the body produces around the clock: sleep, glucose and cardiac electrical activity. Each model in the series learns the dynamics of one signal over time.

The models How the series fits together

Signal traces on this page are schematic illustrations. They are not recorded data or model output.

The series

Three signal models and a potential umbrella

SleepWorld, CGMWorld and ECGWorld each model one physiological signal. PhysioWorld is the potential umbrella model: a unified physiological world model integrating sleep, glucose, and other signals.
