Research agenda

Building clinically useful multimodal AI

My research connects foundation models, medical imaging, reasoning, and reliability across diverse clinical modalities and workflows.

Medical Foundation Models

Unified systems that generalize across tasks, modalities, and clinical settings rather than solving a single narrow problem.

Medical Imaging AI

Learning across radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, microscopy, and other imaging modalities.

Clinical Reasoning

Models that move from observations to grounded diagnostic reasoning, question answering, and report intelligence.

Spatial Grounding

Connecting language and clinical findings to precise regions in medical images.

Trustworthy Medical AI

Robustness, uncertainty, hallucination reduction, and more reliable behavior under realistic clinical variation.

Personalized & Longitudinal AI

Modeling patient-specific and longitudinal information to support personalized healthcare decisions.

Featured project

MedMO — generalist medical AI foundation models

MedMO (4B & 8B) and MedMO-8B-Next unify multimodal understanding, clinical reasoning, and spatial grounding across diverse medical imaging modalities in a single architecture.

Understanding & reasoning: medical VQA, text QA, diagnostic reasoning
Report intelligence: radiology report generation and summarization
Spatial grounding: disease localization with bounding boxes
Cross-modality: radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, microscopy
